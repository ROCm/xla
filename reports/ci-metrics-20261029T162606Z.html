Where the time to signal goes ROCm on DO, NV on Google runners

For each commit, the lane's job that finishes last is followed from the moment the SHA was first triggered. Every second lands in exactly one named phase, and each row adds up to its total (checked in code), so there is no unaccounted time. One row per lane, over all hours of the window.

openxla/xla runs more than one job family in a single workflow, so its lanes get one row per family. Both rows cover the same commits, so they share one n; Total is that family's own signal and Lane signal is the combined one the PR actually waits for - the slower family on each commit, which is why it repeats down a lane. The count beside the family name is how often it was the family that finished last.
